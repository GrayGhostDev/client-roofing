## Critical Reminders

### Execution Priorities
1. **Google Business Profile is #1** - Most impact for local restaurants
2. **Review velocity matters** - Aim for consistent new reviews weekly
3. **Visual content is essential** - Food photography drives engagement
4. **Consistency over perfection** - Regular posting beats sporadic perfection
5. **Track everything** - Baseline metrics enable optimization
