## Market Opportunity

### Competitive Advantages

| Advantage | Market Impact |
|-----------|---------------|
| **100% Halal Certified** | Serves underserved Muslim community in metro Detroit |
| **Build-Your-Own Concept** | Appeals to health-conscious, customization-seeking consumers |
| **Fresh, Made-to-Order** | Quality differentiation from fast food |
| **Strategic Location** | West Bloomfield demographics align with target market |
| **Multi-Platform Delivery** | Maximum reach for convenience-seeking customers |

### Target Market Segments

| Segment | Description | Marketing Approach |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Primary** | Health-conscious professionals (25-45) | Quality, convenience, customization messaging |
| **Secondary** | Families seeking halal options | Family meals, trust, certification emphasis |
| **Tertiary** | Young adults and college students | Social media, value offers, trendy content |

### Market Trends Supporting Growth

- Mediterranean cuisine is #1 trending food category
- Halal food market growing 20% annually
- Build-your-own concepts outperforming traditional fast food
- Delivery continues post-pandemic growth trajectory
