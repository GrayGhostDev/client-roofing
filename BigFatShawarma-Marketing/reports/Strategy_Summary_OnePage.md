## 90-Day Action Plan

### Month 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-4)

**Priority 1: Google Business Profile**
- Complete profile 100% (hours, photos, menu)
- Upload 20+ high-quality food photos
- Post weekly updates (content provided)
- Respond to all reviews within 24 hours

**Priority 2: Review Generation**
- Set up QR code at counter
- Train staff on review request script
- **Goal: 15+ new Google reviews**

**Priority 3: Social Consistency**
- 3-4 Instagram posts per week
- Daily Stories
- 2-3 Reels per week

### Month 2: Amplification (Weeks 5-8)

**Partnership Outreach**
- Contact 5 fitness centers
- Reach out to Muslim Unity Center
- Approach 3 corporate offices
- **Goal: 3 active partnerships**

**Influencer Marketing**
- Contact @detroithalaleats (113K)
- Contact @halalfoodjunkie (114K)
- Invite 3-5 micro-influencers for free meals

### Month 3: Optimization (Weeks 9-12)

**Analyze & Scale**
- Review what's working
- Double down on top channels
- Launch catering program
- **Goal: 50% increase in leads**
