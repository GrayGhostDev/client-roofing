## Review Generation Strategy

### Principles

1. **Never ask for fake reviews** - Only real customers
2. **Don't incentivize reviews** - Against platform policies
3. **Make it easy** - Remove friction from the process
4. **Ask at the right time** - After a positive experience
5. **Be consistent** - Make asking part of the process

### When to Ask

| Moment | Effectiveness | Method |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| After compliment | Very High | Staff verbal ask |
| At checkout | High | Receipt message |
| Post-delivery | High | Follow-up text/email |
| After repeat visit | Medium | Staff relationship |
| Post-catering event | Very High | Follow-up email |

### How to Ask

#### Staff Script (In-Person)

**After positive feedback:**
> "Thank you so much! We really appreciate that. If you have a minute, we'd love it if you could share that on Google. It really helps other people find us. Here's a QR code that takes you right there."

**At checkout:**
> "Thanks for coming in! If you enjoyed your meal, we'd appreciate a Google review. It helps us a lot."

#### Receipt Message
```
Loved your meal? We'd appreciate a review!
Scan to review on Google: [QR Code]
@mybigfatshawarma on Instagram
```

#### Follow-Up Text (Post-Delivery)
```
Hi! Thanks for ordering from My Big Fat Shawarma. We hope you
enjoyed your meal!

If you have a moment, we'd really appreciate a quick Google
review. It helps other food lovers find us.

[Direct Google Review Link]

Thanks for your support!
```

#### Email Template (Post-Catering)
```
Subject: Thank you for choosing My Big Fat Shawarma!

Hi [Name],

Thank you for choosing My Big Fat Shawarma for your recent event.
We hope everyone enjoyed the food!

If the experience met your expectations, we'd be grateful if you
could share your feedback with a Google review. It takes just a
minute and helps us serve more great customers like you.

[Leave a Google Review - Button/Link]

We'd love to serve you again soon!

Warm regards,
The My Big Fat Shawarma Team
```

## Handling Negative Reviews

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Take a breath** - Don't respond emotionally
2. **Read carefully** - Understand the specific complaint
3. **Verify internally** - Check if the complaint is valid
4. **Respond publicly** - Acknowledge and offer to resolve
5. **Take it offline** - Provide contact information
6. **Follow up** - Resolve the issue
7. **Document** - Track for patterns

### Escalation Matrix

| Review Type | Response Level | Action |
|-------------|----------------|--------|
| Minor complaint | Staff | Standard response |
| Service issue | Manager | Response + internal follow-up |
| Food safety concern | Owner | Immediate investigation |
| False/fake review | Owner | Flag for removal |
| Legal threat | Owner | Consult advisor |

### Flagging Fake Reviews

**Google:**
1. Sign into Google Business
2. Find the review
3. Click the three dots
4. Select "Flag as inappropriate"
5. Choose reason

**Criteria for flagging:**
- Never visited (check records)
- Competitor
- Violates guidelines (profanity, hate)
- Spam or irrelevant

## Reputation Recovery

### If Rating Drops Below 4.0

**Immediate Actions (Week 1):**
1. Analyze recent negative reviews for patterns
2. Address any operational issues identified
3. Respond thoughtfully to all negative reviews
4. Launch intensive review generation campaign
5. Alert team to focus on customer experience

**Short-Term (Month 1):**
1. Implement fixes for identified issues
2. Request reviews from satisfied customers
3. Consider promotional offer to encourage return visits
4. Monitor daily for improvement

### Prevention Strategies

- Address complaints immediately
- Empower staff to resolve issues on the spot
- Follow up with unhappy customers
- Track feedback patterns weekly
- Train team on customer experience
