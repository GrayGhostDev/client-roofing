# Content Strategy: My Big Fat Shawarma

*Planning content that builds brand awareness and drives engagement*

---

## Content Strategy Overview

### Goals

1. **Build Brand Awareness** - Establish My Big Fat Shawarma as the go-to halal Mediterranean option
2. **Drive Engagement** - Create content that encourages interaction and sharing
3. **Support SEO** - Develop content that improves search visibility
4. **Educate Customers** - Share the halal story and Mediterranean food culture
5. **Generate Conversions** - Drive orders, visits, and catering inquiries

### Content Pillars

| Pillar | Percentage | Purpose |
|--------|------------|---------|
| **Food Showcase** | 40% | Appetizing content that drives cravings |
| **Behind-the-Scenes** | 20% | Build authenticity and trust |
| **Community** | 20% | Customer features, local connection |
| **Education** | 10% | Halal info, Mediterranean culture |
| **Promotional** | 10% | Offers, catering, delivery |

## Content Types

### Visual Content

#### Photography

| Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Hero Food Shots | Weekly | Showcase signature dishes |
| Ingredient Spreads | Bi-weekly | Highlight freshness |
| Action Shots | Weekly | Show food preparation |
| Customer Photos | As available | Social proof |
| Interior/Exterior | Monthly | Show dining experience |

**Photography Guidelines:**
- Natural lighting preferred
- Bright, warm color tones
- Clean backgrounds (white, wood, marble)
- 45-degree overhead or eye-level angles
- Include garnishes and context

## Platform-Specific Strategy

### Instagram

**Purpose:** Primary visual platform, brand building

| Content Type | Frequency | Notes |
|--------------|-----------|-------|
| Feed Posts | 3-4/week | High-quality food photos/videos |
| Stories | Daily | Behind-the-scenes, polls, daily specials |
| Reels | 2-3/week | Short, engaging video content |
| Lives | Monthly | Kitchen tours, Q&A |

**Best Practices:**
- Post between 11am-1pm and 6pm-8pm
- Use 5-10 relevant hashtags
- Engage with comments within 1 hour
- Share user-generated content to stories

### Facebook

**Purpose:** Community building, events, older demographic

| Content Type | Frequency | Notes |
|--------------|-----------|-------|
| Posts | 2-3/week | Mix of photos, links, community content |
| Events | As needed | Special occasions, promotions |
| Stories | 3-5/week | Cross-post from Instagram |

**Best Practices:**
- Focus on shareable content
- Respond to all comments and messages
- Join and participate in local groups
- Post local community content

## User-Generated Content (UGC)

### Strategy

1. **Encourage sharing** - Table tents, receipts asking for tags
2. **Create shareable moments** - Photo-worthy presentation
3. **Reward sharing** - Feature customers, occasional discounts
4. **Seek permission** - Always ask before reposting

### UGC Prompts

- "Tag us in your bowl for a chance to be featured!"
- "Show us your custom creation @mybigfatshawarma"
- "What's your go-to order? Share it with us!"

### Repurposing UGC

| Source | Use |
|--------|-----|
| Instagram photos | Stories, testimonial posts |
| Positive reviews | Quote graphics, stories |
| Customer videos | Reels compilation |
| Testimonials | Website, Google Business |
