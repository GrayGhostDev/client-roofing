## Facebook Strategy

### Page Optimization

**Page Checklist:**
- [ ] Cover photo: High-quality food or restaurant image
- [ ] Profile photo: Logo
- [ ] About: Complete with all details
- [ ] Hours: Accurate
- [ ] Menu: Uploaded or linked
- [ ] CTA Button: "Order Food" or "Contact Us"

### Content Strategy

| Post Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Food Photos | 2x/week | Visual engagement |
| Community Posts | 1x/week | Local connection |
| Shared Content | 1x/week | Customer features, reviews |
| Events/Offers | As needed | Promotions |

### Facebook Groups Strategy

**Groups to Join:**
- West Bloomfield Community
- West Bloomfield Foodies/Moms
- Oakland County Food Lovers
- Halal Food Michigan
- Metro Detroit Foodies

**Engagement Rules:**
1. Provide value first (answer questions, be helpful)
2. Don't spam or over-promote
3. Only post when relevant (someone asks for restaurant recs)
4. Be a helpful community member
5. Build relationships over time

### Facebook-Specific Content

| Content Type | Works Well | Avoid |
|--------------|------------|-------|
| Customer reviews | High engagement | Over-promotion |
| Community content | Shareable | Hard sell posts |
| Behind-scenes | Authentic feel | Low-quality images |
| Event announcements | Information sharing | Too-frequent promos |

## User-Generated Content Strategy

### Encouraging UGC

**In-Store:**
- Table tent: "Tag @mybigfatshawarma"
- Receipt message: "Share your meal!"
- Staff reminder at pickup

**On Social:**
- "Tag us for a feature!"
- Repost customer content
- Create shareable moments

### UGC Permission Process

1. Screenshot post
2. DM: "We love your photo! Mind if we share?"
3. Wait for permission
4. Share with credit

### Reposting Template

```
This looks amazing! Thanks for sharing, @[username]!

What's your go-to order? Tell us below.
```

## Crisis Management

### Response Protocol

| Situation | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| Negative review/comment | Acknowledge, apologize, take offline |
| Viral complaint | Immediate response, involve management |
| Food safety concern | Take seriously, investigate, respond |
| Incorrect information | Correct politely with facts |

### Response Template (Negative)

```
We're really sorry to hear about your experience, [Name]. This isn't
the standard we hold ourselves to. Please reach out to us at
(248) 977-4778 or DM us so we can make this right. We value your
feedback and want to fix this.
```
