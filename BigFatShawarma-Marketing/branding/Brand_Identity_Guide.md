# Brand Identity Guide: My Big Fat Shawarma

*Defining how we look, sound, and connect with our community*

---

## Brand Overview

### Brand Essence

**Who We Are:** A family-oriented, 100% halal Mediterranean fast-casual restaurant that brings authentic flavors to West Bloomfield through fresh, customizable meals.

**What We Promise:** Fresh, halal-certified Mediterranean food made your way, every time.

**Why It Matters:** We serve a community that deserves delicious, trustworthy halal options without compromising on taste, quality, or convenience.

---

## Brand Positioning

### Positioning Statement

**My Big Fat Shawarma** is a fast-casual Mediterranean restaurant for halal-seeking families and health-conscious diners in West Bloomfield who want authentic, fresh, customizable meals. Unlike generic fast-casual chains, we offer 100% halal-certified food with true Mediterranean flavors and complete personalization.

### Brand Pillars

| Pillar | Description | Expression |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| **Authenticity** | Real Mediterranean recipes and flavors | "Authentic flavors from family recipes" |
| **Trust** | 100% halal certified, no exceptions | "Certified halal - no compromises" |
| **Freshness** | Made-to-order with quality ingredients | "Fresh, never frozen, made for you" |
| **Community** | Local, family-owned, community-focused | "Your neighborhood shawarma spot" |

---

## Brand Voice

### Voice Characteristics

| Characteristic | Description | Example |
|----------------|-------------|---------|
| **Warm** | Friendly and welcoming | "Welcome to the family!" |
| **Authentic** | Genuine and honest | "Real recipes, real flavors" |
| **Confident** | Proud of our quality | "The best shawarma in West Bloomfield" |
| **Approachable** | Never pretentious | "Good food, good people" |
| **Inviting** | Makes you want to visit | "Your table is ready" |

### Tone by Context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| **Social Media** | Playful, engaging | "Bowl goals! What's in yours?" |
| **Customer Service** | Warm, helpful | "We'd love to make this right for you" |
| **Marketing** | Confident, inviting | "Taste the difference halal makes" |
| **Formal (catering)** | Professional, warm | "Let us cater your next event" |

### Voice Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Use "we" and "you" | Use corporate jargon |
| Be conversational | Be stuffy or formal |
| Share genuine enthusiasm | Over-hype or exaggerate |
| Celebrate community | Be exclusionary |
| Highlight freshness and quality | Focus on price/discounts |

## Brand Guidelines Summary

### Always Do

- [ ] Use the logo correctly with proper spacing
- [ ] Maintain consistent colors across all materials
- [ ] Write in the brand voice (warm, authentic)
- [ ] Highlight halal certification prominently
- [ ] Use high-quality, appetizing food photography
- [ ] Include contact information and social handles
- [ ] Respond to customers with warmth

### Never Do

- [ ] Alter the logo (stretch, recolor, rotate)
- [ ] Use off-brand colors or fonts
- [ ] Write in corporate or cold language
- [ ] Compete primarily on price
- [ ] Use low-quality or dark food photos
- [ ] Ignore customer feedback or reviews
- [ ] Make claims we can't substantiate
