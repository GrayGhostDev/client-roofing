## Review Strategy

### Importance
- 93% of consumers read online reviews before visiting
- Businesses with 4.5+ stars receive 28% more clicks
- Review velocity (new reviews per month) affects rankings

### Review Generation System

#### Timing
Best time to request reviews: Within 24 hours of positive experience

#### Methods

| Method | Implementation | Expected Response |
|--------|----------------|-------------------|
| Receipt QR Code | Print QR linking to Google review | 2-3% |
| Follow-up Text | Automated after order | 5-8% |
| Email Request | Post-order email sequence | 3-5% |
| Staff Ask | Verbal request at register | 10-15% |
| Table Tent | In-store review reminder | 1-2% |

#### Review Request Template (Text/Email)
```
Hi [Name], thank you for choosing My Big Fat Shawarma!

We hope you enjoyed your meal. If you have a moment, we'd love to hear
about your experience. Your review helps us serve you better and helps
others discover our food.

[Leave a Google Review] - [direct link]

Thank you for your support!
- The My Big Fat Shawarma Team
```

### Review Response Templates

#### Positive Review Response
```
Thank you so much for the wonderful review, [Name]! We're thrilled you
enjoyed your [specific item if mentioned]. Our team takes pride in
[relevant quality - fresh ingredients/authentic flavors/etc.].
We can't wait to serve you again soon!
```

#### Neutral Review Response
```
Thank you for your feedback, [Name]. We appreciate you taking the time
to share your experience. We're always looking to improve - [address
specific point if applicable]. We'd love the opportunity to exceed
your expectations next time!
```

#### Negative Review Response
```
[Name], thank you for bringing this to our attention. We're sorry your
experience didn't meet expectations. This isn't the standard we hold
ourselves to. Please reach out to us at (248) 977-4778 so we can make
this right. We value your feedback and hope to earn back your trust.
```

### Review Targets

| Timeframe | Target New Reviews | Platform Focus |
|-----------|--------------------|----------------|
| Month 1 | 15+ | Google (primary), Yelp |
| Month 2 | 20+ | Google, Yelp, Facebook |
| Month 3 | 25+ | All platforms |
| Ongoing | 8-10/month | Maintain velocity |
