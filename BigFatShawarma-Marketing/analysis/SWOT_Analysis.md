## Competitive Positioning Statement

**My Big Fat Shawarma** is the premier halal Mediterranean fast-casual destination in West Bloomfield, offering build-your-own bowls, wraps, and salads made fresh with authentic recipes. Unlike generic fast-casual chains, we provide 100% halal-certified options with true Mediterranean flavors and complete customization.

### Key Differentiators
1. **Halal Certified** - Serving the underserved Muslim community
2. **Build-Your-Own** - Complete customization for all dietary needs
3. **Mediterranean Authenticity** - Real recipes, not Americanized versions
4. **Local Focus** - Community-centered, locally owned
