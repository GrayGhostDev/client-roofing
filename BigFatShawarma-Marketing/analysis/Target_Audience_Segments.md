## Primary Segment: Halal-Seeking Families

### Demographics

| Characteristic | Profile |
|----------------|---------|
| **Age Range** | 30-55 (parents) |
| **Household** | Families with children |
| **Religion** | Muslim (practicing) |
| **Income** | $60,000-$150,000+ |
| **Location** | West Bloomfield, Farmington Hills, Troy, surrounding areas |
| **Occupation** | Professionals, business owners, healthcare |

### Psychographics

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Values** | Faith, family, community, quality |
| **Pain Points** | Limited halal dining options, concern about certification authenticity |
| **Lifestyle** | Busy families balancing work and home |
| **Media Consumption** | Facebook, WhatsApp groups, community events |
| **Decision Drivers** | Halal certification, family-friendly, convenience |

### Behavioral Patterns

| Behavior | Pattern |
|----------|---------|
| **Dining Frequency** | 2-4x monthly at restaurants |
| **Average Spend** | $50-80 per family visit |
| **Discovery** | Word of mouth, community recommendations |
| **Loyalty** | High when trust is established |
| **Order Type** | Dine-in for occasions, delivery for convenience |

### Marketing Implications

| Channel | Strategy |
|---------|----------|
| **Facebook Groups** | Join halal/Muslim community groups |
| **Community** | Partner with mosques, Islamic centers |
| **Word of Mouth** | Referral program, review requests |
| **Messaging** | Lead with halal certification prominently |
| **Offers** | Family meal deals, Ramadan specials |

### Segment-Specific Messaging

> "100% Halal certified. Family-friendly. Fresh Mediterranean favorites your whole family will love."
