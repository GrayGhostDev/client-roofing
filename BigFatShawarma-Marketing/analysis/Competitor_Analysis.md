## Competitive Differentiation Strategy

### Primary Differentiator: Halal Certification

**Messaging:**
- "West Bloomfield's Only 100% Halal Mediterranean"
- "Certified Halal - No Compromises"
- "Halal Done Right"

**Execution:**
- Prominently display certification
- Include in all marketing materials
- Target halal-seeking communities
- Partner with mosques/Islamic centers

### Secondary Differentiator: Local Authenticity

**Messaging:**
- "Family-Owned Mediterranean Kitchen"
- "Real Recipes, Real Flavors"
- "Your Local Shawarma Spot"

**Execution:**
- Share owner/family story
- Highlight recipe origins
- Community involvement
- Local partnerships

### Tertiary Differentiator: Customization + Fresh

**Messaging:**
- "Built Your Way, Fresh Every Time"
- "100% Customizable, 100% Fresh"

**Execution:**
- Emphasize made-to-order process
- Show ingredient freshness
- Dietary accommodation flexibility

- Strong halal differentiator in underserved market

1. **Own the halal positioning** - Non-negotiable differentiator
