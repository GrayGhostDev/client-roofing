## Indirect Competitors

### National Fast-Casual Chains

#### CAVA

| Factor | Details | Competitive Implication |
|--------|---------|------------------------|
| **Locations** | Expanding in Michigan | Growing threat |
| **Halal Status** | Not certified | Advantage for MBFS |
| **Menu** | Mediterranean bowls/pitas | Similar concept |
| **Price Point** | $12-16 | Comparable |
| **Brand Recognition** | High (national) | Marketing challenge |
| **Digital Presence** | Strong | Benchmark for MBFS |

**CAVA SWOT vs. My Big Fat Shawarma:**
- *Their Strength:* Brand recognition, marketing budget
- *Their Weakness:* No halal certification, less authentic
- *Our Opportunity:* Halal market segment, local authenticity
- *Our Threat:* Customer acquisition cost, location convenience

#### Chipotle

| Factor | Details | Competitive Implication |
|--------|---------|------------------------|
| **Locations** | Multiple in area | Convenience competition |
| **Halal Status** | Not certified | Advantage for MBFS |
| **Menu** | Mexican-focused | Different cuisine |
| **Price Point** | $10-15 | Similar |
| **Brand Recognition** | Very High | Indirect competition |
| **Digital/Loyalty** | Strong rewards program | Benchmark |

**Competitive Notes:**
- Competes for "fast-casual bowl" occasions
- Different cuisine reduces direct comparison
- Their loyalty program is benchmark-worthy

## Competitive Differentiation Strategy

### Primary Differentiator: Halal Certification

**Messaging:**
- "West Bloomfield's Only 100% Halal Mediterranean"
- "Certified Halal - No Compromises"
- "Halal Done Right"

**Execution:**
- Prominently display certification
- Include in all marketing materials
- Target halal-seeking communities
- Partner with mosques/Islamic centers

### Secondary Differentiator: Local Authenticity

**Messaging:**
- "Family-Owned Mediterranean Kitchen"
- "Real Recipes, Real Flavors"
- "Your Local Shawarma Spot"

**Execution:**
- Share owner/family story
- Highlight recipe origins
- Community involvement
- Local partnerships

### Tertiary Differentiator: Customization + Fresh

**Messaging:**
- "Built Your Way, Fresh Every Time"
- "100% Customizable, 100% Fresh"

**Execution:**
- Emphasize made-to-order process
- Show ingredient freshness
- Dietary accommodation flexibility

## Competitive Summary

### Current Position
- Strong halal differentiator in underserved market
- Building brand awareness as newer business
- Multi-platform delivery creates convenience parity

### Strategic Focus
1. **Own the halal positioning** - Non-negotiable differentiator
2. **Build review volume** - Reduce "unknown" factor
3. **Strengthen local presence** - Community over brand
4. **Compete on experience** - Not on price

### Key Competitive Principle

> "We don't compete with CAVA or Chipotle on their terms. We serve a specific community with authentic, halal Mediterranean food. Our competition is customer unawareness, not other restaurants."
