## Partnership Strategy Overview

### Why Partnerships Matter

| Benefit | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| **Low-cost marketing** | Reach new audiences without ad spend |
| **Built-in credibility** | Partner endorsement = trust |
| **Community presence** | Become part of local fabric |
| **Recurring revenue** | Catering and corporate accounts |
| **Word-of-mouth** | Organic referrals |

### Partnership Categories

| Category | Examples | Priority |
|----------|----------|----------|
| **Health/Fitness** | Gyms, fitness studios, wellness centers | High |
| **Community** | Mosques, schools, youth sports | High |
| **Corporate** | Local businesses, office parks | High |
| **Events** | Wedding planners, event venues | Medium |
| **Complementary Businesses** | Non-competing food, retail | Medium |

## Ambassador Program

### Concept

Recruit community members who love the restaurant to be informal ambassadors, spreading word-of-mouth in exchange for perks.

### Ambassador Benefits

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Personal discount** | 20% off all orders |
| **Referral credit** | $5 for each referral |
| **Exclusive access** | New items, events |
| **Recognition** | Social media features |

### Ambassador Expectations

- Share positive experiences on social media
- Recommend to friends and family
- Provide feedback and suggestions
- Represent brand positively

### Recruiting Ambassadors

| Source | Approach |
|--------|----------|
| Loyal customers | Personal invitation |
| Social followers | DM outreach |
| Community leaders | Direct request |
| Influencers | Formal partnership |
