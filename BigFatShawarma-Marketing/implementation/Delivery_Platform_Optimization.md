## Photo Strategy for All Platforms

### Required Photos

| Photo Type | Quantity | Specifications |
|------------|----------|----------------|
| Cover/Hero | 1 | 16:9 ratio, best dish |
| Logo | 1 | Square, high-res |
| Menu Items | All items | Square or 4:3 |
| Lifestyle | 3-5 | Dining/ordering context |

### Photo Guidelines

**Technical Specs:**
- Resolution: 1200x800 pixels minimum
- Format: JPEG or PNG
- File size: Under 5MB
- Lighting: Bright, natural preferred

**Styling Tips:**
1. Clean plate edges
2. Garnish for color
3. Show ingredients clearly
4. Steam/sizzle for hot items
5. Fresh, abundant appearance

### Priority Items for Photography

## Pricing Strategy

### Platform Fee Consideration

| Platform | Typical Fee | Pricing Approach |
|----------|-------------|------------------|
| DoorDash | 15-30% | Match or slight markup |
| Uber Eats | 15-30% | Match or slight markup |
| Grubhub | 15-30% | Match or slight markup |

### Recommended Approach

| Strategy | Pros | Cons |
|----------|------|------|
| **Match dine-in** | Customer-friendly | Lower margins |
| **10-15% markup** | Covers some fees | May lose price-sensitive |
| **Higher markup** | Maintains margins | May hurt volume |

**Recommendation:** Start with matching dine-in prices, monitor margins, adjust as needed.

### Value Optimization

| Tactic | Implementation |
|--------|----------------|
| **Meal Combos** | Bundle entree + side + drink at slight discount |
| **Family Meals** | 4-person meals at attractive price point |
| **Add-Ons** | Prominent side/drink suggestions |
| **Premium Options** | Extra protein, premium toppings |

## Operations Optimization

### Prep Time Accuracy

| Issue | Impact | Solution |
|-------|--------|----------|
| Times too short | Late orders, complaints | Set realistic times |
| Times too long | Customers choose others | Optimize for accuracy |

**Best Practice:** Set prep time slightly higher than actual, deliver faster than promised.

### Packaging

| Consideration | Best Practice |
|---------------|---------------|
| **Temperature** | Separate hot/cold items |
| **Leakage** | Secure lids, double-bag sauces |
| **Presentation** | Branded stickers, napkins |
| **Instructions** | Include reheating tips |

## Review Management on Platforms

### Platform Review Response

| Platform | Response Importance | Response Time |
|----------|---------------------|---------------|
| DoorDash | High | 24 hours |
| Uber Eats | High | 24 hours |
| Grubhub | Medium | 48 hours |

### Common Issues and Responses

| Issue | Response | Prevention |
|-------|----------|------------|
| Missing items | Apologize, offer resolution | Double-check orders |
| Cold food | Apologize, improve packaging | Better insulation |
| Long delivery | Apologize (often not your fault) | Accurate prep times |
| Quality concerns | Apologize, offer to make right | QC before handoff |
