## Risk Mitigation

### Potential Challenges

| Challenge | Mitigation |
|-----------|------------|
| Slow review growth | Intensify ask process, try different methods |
| Low ad performance | Test new creative, adjust targeting |
| Limited budget | Focus on organic strategies |
| Capacity constraints | Address before scaling marketing |
| Negative review impact | Implement crisis response quickly |

### Success Factors

1. **Consistency** - Execute daily/weekly tasks
2. **Measurement** - Track everything
3. **Flexibility** - Adjust based on data
4. **Focus** - Don't try to do everything at once
5. **Quality** - Never sacrifice food/service for marketing
