# 30-Day Quick Wins: My Big Fat Shawarma

*High-impact, low-cost actions for immediate results*

## Overview

This playbook outlines the most impactful marketing actions to complete in the first 30 days. Focus on these proven tactics before expanding to more complex strategies.

**Philosophy:** Do the basics exceptionally well before adding complexity.

**Time Required:** 2-4 hours (includes photo taking)
**Cost:** Free to $200 (professional photography optional)
**Impact:** High

#### Photo Requirements

| Category | Minimum | Priority Items |
|----------|---------|----------------|

| Exterior | 3 | Storefront, signage |
| Team | 3 | Staff preparing food |
| Logo | 1 | High-resolution |

#### DIY Photo Tips
1. Use natural light (near windows, 11am-2pm)
2. Clean the plate edges
3. Use white or neutral backgrounds
4. Shoot from 45-degree angle
5. Include garnishes for color
6. Avoid flash photography

### Day 18-19: Review Push Campaign

**Time Required:** 2 hours
**Cost:** Free
**Impact:** High

#### Goal: 10 new Google reviews in one week

**Tactics:**
1. **Personal outreach** - Text/email loyal customers
2. **In-store push** - Staff asks every customer
3. **Social media ask** - Post requesting reviews
4. **Family and friends** - Authentic experiences only

**Personal Outreach Template:**
```
Hi [Name]! This is [Owner] from My Big Fat Shawarma.

We're working on growing our local presence and reviews really
help. If you've enjoyed our food, would you mind leaving us a
quick Google review?

Here's the direct link: [link]

Thank you so much for your support!
```

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

1. **Inconsistent NAP** - Triple check address format everywhere
2. **Asking for fake reviews** - Only request from real customers
3. **Ignoring negative reviews** - Always respond professionally
4. **Posting without photos** - Visual content performs 10x better
5. **Giving up too early** - SEO takes 60-90 days to show results
