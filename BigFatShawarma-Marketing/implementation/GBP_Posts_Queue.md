## GBP Post Best Practices

### Do's
- Post 1-2 times per week consistently
- Always include high-quality images
- Use clear calls-to-action
- Mention location and ordering options
- Keep text scannable (short paragraphs)

### Don'ts
- Don't use all caps (except for emphasis)
- Don't include phone numbers in the text (use the button)
- Don't post more than 3x per week
- Don't use stock photos (authentic food shots only)
- Don't forget to mark posts as completed

### Image Requirements
- Minimum: 720 x 720 pixels
- Recommended: 1200 x 900 pixels
- Format: JPG or PNG
- File size: Under 5MB
