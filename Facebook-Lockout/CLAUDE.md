**Client:** iSwitch Roofs
**Issue:** Facebook Business Account Lockout
**Created:** 2026-02-05
**Status:** Recovery In Progress

## Overview

This document outlines legitimate recovery options for regaining access to a locked Facebook Business account. All methods go through official Meta/Facebook channels.

---

## Immediate Actions

### 1. Identify the Type of Lockout

| Lockout Type | Symptoms | Primary Recovery Path |
|--------------|----------|----------------------|
| **Password Issue** | Can't log in, password not accepted | Password reset |
| **2FA Lockout** | Can't receive/enter 2FA code | 2FA recovery or trusted contacts |
| **Account Disabled** | Message says account disabled | Appeals process |
| **Hacked Account** | Unauthorized changes, can't access | Hacked account recovery |
| **Business Manager Removed** | Lost admin access to Business Suite | Contact remaining admins or Meta support |
| **Policy Violation** | Account restricted for policy reasons | Policy appeal process |

## Recovery Methods (By Priority)

### Method 1: Standard Password Recovery

**URL:** https://www.facebook.com/login/identify

**Steps:**
1. Go to Facebook login page
2. Click "Forgot password?"
3. Enter email, phone, or username associated with account
4. Choose recovery method (email or SMS)
5. Enter code received
6. Create new password

**Timeline:** Immediate (if email/phone accessible)

---

### Method 2: Two-Factor Authentication Recovery

**If you lost access to your 2FA device:**

**Option A: Use Backup Codes**
- If backup codes were saved during 2FA setup, use one to log in
- Location: Should have been downloaded when 2FA was enabled

**Option B: Use Another Logged-In Device**
- Check if still logged in on another phone, tablet, or computer
- Go to Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication
- Generate new codes or disable 2FA temporarily

**Option C: Trusted Contacts Recovery**
- If configured, request recovery codes from trusted contacts
- URL: https://www.facebook.com/recover/initiate

**Option D: Identity Verification**
- Facebook may ask for government ID to verify identity
- Upload clear photo of valid ID matching account name

---

### Method 3: Hacked Account Recovery

**URL:** https://www.facebook.com/hacked

**Steps:**
1. Visit the hacked account page
2. Click "My Account Is Compromised"
3. Enter email or phone number
4. Enter current OR old password (even if changed by hacker)
5. Follow identity verification steps
6. Secure account once access restored

**Additional Steps After Recovery:**
- [ ] Change password immediately
- [ ] Review and remove unrecognized devices
- [ ] Check for unauthorized app connections
- [ ] Enable 2FA with new device
- [ ] Review recent account activity

---

### Method 4: Disabled Account Appeal

**URL:** https://www.facebook.com/help/contact/260749603972907

**When to Use:** Account shows "Your account has been disabled"

**Steps:**
1. Fill out the appeal form completely
2. Use the email address associated with the disabled account
3. Provide clear, honest explanation
4. Upload government-issued ID if requested
5. Wait for response (can take 1-30 days)

**Tips for Successful Appeal:**
- Be polite and professional
- Explain any misunderstandings clearly
- Don't submit multiple appeals (delays processing)
- Check spam folder for responses

---

### Method 5: Business Manager/Business Suite Recovery

**URL:** https://business.facebook.com/help

**Scenario A: Removed as Admin**
- Contact another admin of the Business Manager to re-add you
- If no other admins exist, proceed to Scenario B

**Scenario B: No Admin Access Available**
- Go to: https://www.facebook.com/help/contact/179049432194862
- Submit Business Manager access request
- Provide business documentation:
  - Business license
  - Tax documents
  - Proof of business ownership
  - Domain verification (if applicable)

**Scenario C: Business Verification Issues**
- Complete business verification: https://business.facebook.com/settings/security
- Required documents may include:
  - Articles of incorporation
  - Business utility bill
  - Business bank statement
  - Tax registration certificate

---

### Method 6: Contact Meta Business Support Directly

**For Verified Business Accounts:**

1. **Meta Business Help Center**
   - URL: https://www.facebook.com/business/help
   - Click "Get Support" or "Contact Us"
   - Select issue category
   - Choose chat or email support

2. **Meta Business Support Chat**
   - Available for accounts with active ad spend
   - Access through Business Suite > Help > Contact Support

3. **Meta for Business Contact Form**
   - URL: https://www.facebook.com/help/contact/540aborar
   - Select "I can't access my account"

**For Ad Accounts with Spending History:**
- Accounts with significant ad spend often get priority support
- Reference previous ad account IDs and spending history

## Escalation Options

If standard recovery fails after 2+ weeks:

### 1. Social Media Outreach
- **Twitter/X:** @MetaforBusiness, @Meta
- Post publicly describing issue (no sensitive details)
- Sometimes gets faster response from support team

### 2. Better Business Bureau
- File complaint at https://www.bbb.org
- Search for "Meta Platforms, Inc."
- Formal complaints sometimes escalate internally

### 3. State Attorney General
- For business accounts, some state AGs have helped with tech platform disputes
- Last resort option

### 4. Meta Verified Subscription
- If able to create new account or access another
- Meta Verified ($11.99/month) provides direct support access
- May help expedite recovery of related business assets

## Prevention for Future

Once access is restored, implement these safeguards:

### Immediate Actions
- [ ] Enable two-factor authentication (use authenticator app, not SMS)
- [ ] Save backup codes in secure location
- [ ] Add 3-5 trusted contacts for recovery
- [ ] Verify email and phone are current
- [ ] Remove old/unused devices from account

### Business Account Security
- [ ] Add multiple admins to Business Manager (minimum 2-3)
- [ ] Use Business Manager roles appropriately (not everyone needs admin)
- [ ] Complete business verification
- [ ] Set up login alerts
- [ ] Review connected apps quarterly
- [ ] Document all admin credentials securely

### Password Best Practices
- [ ] Use unique password (not used elsewhere)
- [ ] Use password manager
- [ ] Change password every 6-12 months
- [ ] Never share login credentials
